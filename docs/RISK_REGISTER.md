## Definitions

- **Likelihood:** Low = unlikely absent specific action; Medium = plausible given normal operation; High = will occur with normal use; Occurred = already happened.
- **Impact:** Low = minor inconvenience; Medium = operational disruption or data quality issue; High = financial loss, security breach, or credibility damage.
- **Status:** OPEN = no meaningful control; PARTIAL = controls exist but gaps remain; MITIGATED = risk acceptably reduced; CLOSED = resolved with verified evidence.
